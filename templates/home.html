Comprehensive Vital Signs Tracking

Monitors Heart Rate, Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO2), Body Temperature, Blood Pressure, and estimated Respiratory Rate using non-invasive sensors.

ARIMA Predictive Health Trends

Leverages Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) time-series forecasting on historical health records to predict vital sign trends and give early warnings before health deteriorates.

Live Feed & Visual Monitoring

Integrated ESP32-CAM streams live snapshots to help remote caregivers observe the user's environment in real-time.

Smart Automated Medicine Dispenser

Combines a Real-Time Clock (RTC) module and servo motors to sound reminders and automatically dispense prescribed medications at exact scheduled times.

Green-Red Risk Assessment & SMS Alerts

Categorizes physiological condition using an intuitive Green (Normal) vs. Red (Critical) status. Automatically dispatches immediate emergency SMS alerts via GSM to family or caregivers during critical health events.

Research-Grade Data Logging

Stores and logs continuous physiological data for historical analysis, medical consultations, and academic research in IoT preventive healthcare.

About Project ARUGA

ARUGA — Development of an Intelligent Preventive Healthcare System with Smart Medication Dispensing, Continuous Vital Sign Monitoring, and Early Health Warning — is a research initiative developed at Muntinlupa National High School (STEM Strand).

As the number of older adults living independently grows, many face challenges in tracking chronic conditions, remembering complex medication routines, and recognizing early health shifts. ARUGA solves this by combining an IoT-based assistive companion robot, a wearable sensor wristband, and machine learning analytics into a single affordable ecosystem.

By analyzing continuous physiological data with ARIMA time-series forecasting, ARUGA moves healthcare from reactive treatment to proactive prevention—detecting subtle physiological changes before they turn into critical emergencies.

This platform and prototype are currently under active development. We welcome collaboration from healthcare professionals, data scientists, and robotics engineers interested in advancing smart, independent healthcare.

How ARUGA Works

Wearable Wristband Unit

Powered by an ESP32 micro-controller, incorporating MAX30102 light sensors and temperature modules for non-invasive health tracking.

Companion Robot Base

Driven by an Arduino Mega 2560 managing the physical medicine dispenser, LCD display, local buzzers, and ESP32-CAM.

GSM Emergency Gateway

Uses a SIM800L module to ensure critical alerts reach caregivers instantly via SMS, even without active home internet.
